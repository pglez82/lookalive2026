About the Boat

The Westerly Centaur 26 is a cherished English cruising yacht, built with solid craftsmanship and designed for comfortable coastal and blue-water sailing. With a shallow 0.9 m draft and twin keels, she can safely beach in hidden coves and reach anchorages deeper-draft boats cannot.

We bought Look Alive in Burriana, Spain, in 2019 and sailed her to Greece in 2020. She spent wonderful summers exploring the Ionian Sea before this season setting off on a new adventure around the Mediterranean — Italy and Sicily.

She offers surprising internal height — you can stand up inside (up to ~1.8 m), making life aboard comfortable for extended cruising.
